# Zaynr Ecosystem

Plugins for Paper servers, built around clean and modern player and staff experiences.

## Projects

- **[Zelly's Guard](https://zaynr-ecosystem.github.io/guard/)**: land protection and region management for Paper servers.
- **[Zelly's Nexus](https://zaynr-ecosystem.github.io/nexus/)**: the companion suite for moderation, utility and social tooling.
- **[BlockProt Reloaded](https://zaynr-ecosystem.github.io/blockprot/)**: a fork of BlockProt with fixes and modern Paper support.
